# Research Roadmap

## v0.2
- mode-aware attack-vs-delay discrimination;
- rewritten-replay detection using temporal change statistics;
- explicit occupancy/train cross-sensor diagnosis;
- probabilistic state reconstruction;
- detection-delay and source-isolation metrics.

## v0.3
- larger network graphs and timetable recovery;
- multiple junctions and route reservations;
- uncertainty-aware hybrid diagnosis;
- formalized safety property checking for the simulator.

## Later
- adapters to established railway simulation tools or public research datasets, subject to licensing and a strict non-operational safety boundary.
